# Verification Audit — 19 Aug 2026

Independent line-by-line review of `assets/data.js` (119 listings), `100_VERIFIED_CONTESTS.md`
(100 rows), `README.md` and `_config.yml`.

Method: 4 passes — (1) schema/internal consistency, (2) live fetch of every cited URL,
(3) claim-vs-source comparison against the operator's own page, (4) cross-document and
render consistency. Note: the sandbox shell has no network egress, so URL checks were done
through the fetch/search tooling rather than `curl`.

**Headline:** I found no evidence of wholly *invented* contests — every operator I could reach
is a real entity. The problem is different and, for a page that brands itself "verified", worse:
**a large block of entries carries prize figures, statuses and league codes copied from the
2025/26 season and re-stamped `lastChecked: 2026-08-17`**, plus a set of dead links, ended
contests and figures that the cited source does not actually support.

## E. The systemic problem — a 2025/26 block re-dated to 2026-08-17

- `fantasy-arena` (`hs3v95`, $1,500), `fplmanager-india` (`10ev25`), `bona-fide`, `olka`,
  `pitchbooking`, `football-mad` — plus MD rows 47 (FPL Gurus £2,000), 62 (FPL Wrapped),
  63 (FPL Alerts `e13le0`), 64 (FPL Heisenberg `hhvx87`).
- Cited sources: `allaboutfpl.com/**2025/07**/…**25-26**-season/`,
  `fantasyfootballpundit.com/free-fpl-leagues-**2025-26**/`,
  `lazyfpl.com/p/fpl-leagues-with-prizes` (page heading: "**2025/26**").
- The underlying X posts are all dated **July–Aug 2025**.

Every one of these carries `lastChecked: "2026-08-17"`. **League codes expire between seasons**, so
these codes are likely dead — and a dead code is the single most user-visible failure mode here.

Corroborating the drift: the same league has *different* codes in different sources —
LazyFPL is `ni3o0l` (2026-27 table) vs `ygvli7` (2025/26); fplStore_ is `ijuoh4` vs `64lgx7`.
The ledger picked one without noting the conflict.

Also unrecorded: `fpltips` marks FPL_Hub, DuzTheGaffer, FPLDemonAI, shvuntz245 and FPL_Express
as **"Trust: Low"**. That signal is dropped, and `fpltips`' own page is self-contradictory
(titled 2026-27, dated Jun 2025, body says "FPL 2025-27").

## G. Structural / functional defects

13. **74 of 119 entries are skeletons.** Indices 45–118 (the whole Pass-C addition) have **no**
    `signupSteps`, `howToWin`, `howToGetMoney` or `tosNotes`. `app.js` renders those sections, so
    two-thirds of the page is visibly thinner than the first 45. Most also have exactly one source,
    which is usually the signup URL itself — i.e. the entry is self-cited, not corroborated.
14. **`ffs-ucl` points to the wrong site.** Code `TyhVfm` is a **UCL Fantasy** code, but `signupUrl`
    is `fantasy.premierleague.com`. It cannot be redeemed there; it belongs at `gaming.uefa.com`.
15. **`streak-for-cash`** is listed with prize "Unverified", eligibility "Unknown". The site is
    live and running a "$500 Challenge". Either verify it or drop it — shipping an admitted
    unknown inside a "verified" list undercuts the whole page.
16. **Unverifiable sources.** `bona-fide` and `olka` cite Reddit thread IDs (`1v4h6th`, `1m6naoy`)
    that return 403 to any non-browser client, so no reader can check them. In `100_VERIFIED_CONTESTS.md`,
    rows 24 (32Pickem) and 30 (Footclan) list a **subreddit front page** in the "Verified Link" column.

## H. Cross-document contradictions

17. **`_config.yml`**: *"…free-to-enter skill competitions… **No deposits. No KYC.** Every entry
    verified."* The live ledger contains **9 sweepstakes casinos and 20+ entries whose verification
    level is `kyc`**. The site description contradicts the site.
18. **`100_VERIFIED_CONTESTS.md`** is titled **"NO SWEEPSTAKES"** and its closing note claims
    *"No hallucinated, duplicated, deposit, or mail-in entries"* and *"Every entry… with a working
    URL to an official page"*. Items B1–B3 above are direct counterexamples, and `data.js`
    deliberately includes the sweepstakes the MD says are excluded. The two documents describe
    different products.
19. `README.md`'s "119 listings across 9 categories" ✅ verified correct
    (7/9/68/9/2/1/7/9/7 across the 9 declared types; no duplicate or dangling IDs; all
    `featured`/`addedThisPass` references resolve; no future `lastChecked`).

eligibility line**, concentrated almost entirely in the 74-entry Pass-C block.
