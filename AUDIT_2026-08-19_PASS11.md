## Entries added — source, tier, and what was verified

### 1. Betfred Super League Fantasy — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `fantasy.superleague.co.uk/prizes-winners` (official).
- **Verified:** "Free to Play." Monthly winners choose a PS5/Xbox + games (up to £600); season winner
  gets four hospitality tickets, annual SuperLeague+ pass, launch invite and a signed shirt.
  Non-cash; sponsored by Betfred (no betting account required).

### 2. Betfred Super League Predictor — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `superleague.co.uk/article/5728/play-super-league-predictor-in-2026` (official).
- **Verified:** "FREE, season-long" prediction game. Prizes: season winner launch invite + 4 event
  tickets + SuperLeague+ pass; monthly/weekly tickets; streak prizes (18/24/30/36). Non-cash.

### 3. MotoGP Fantasy (Official) — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `motogp.com/en/news/2026/02/18/motogp-fantasy-play-smart-and-win-big/874823` (official).
- **Verified:** free; Official MotoGP League prizes = signed Marc Márquez helmet (1st), PS5 +
  MotoGP26 (2nd), signed knee slider (3rd); weekly GP winner gets two tickets. Non-cash.

### 4. The Hundred Fantasy (ECB) — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `thehundred.com/terms/fantasy` (official).
- **Verified:** "free to enter and no purchase is necessary." Prizes (UK residents): 1st signed bat;
  2nd Citizen watch + 4 tickets; block winners get Final 2026 tickets. Non-cash.

### 5. INDYCAR Fantasy Challenge — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `indycar.com/fantasychallenge/indycar/fullrules` (official).
- **Verified:** "free to play" (on GridRival). Grand prize = 2026 Indy 500 VIP experience (ARV
  $5,000); 2nd memorabilia ($650); 3rd–10th packages. US residents only. Non-cash.

### 6. Fantasy Super Rugby Pacific — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `playfantasyrugby.com/rugby-championship/help/game-guidelines` (official).
- **Verified:** "THE OFFICIAL FANTASY GAME… FREE to play and you can win up to $10,000." Overall
  winner gets USD $10,000 (AUD $14,000) major prize. The list's second genuinely free cash fantasy
  prize after the prop-firm competitions.

### 7. Fantasy Gaelic Football (GAA) — `live`, `full` ✅ (official account)
- **Source:** `fantasygaelicfootball.com` + `x.com/fantasygaelicfb` (official).
- **Verified:** free-to-play Championship fantasy; the 2026 champion won £700 first prize and the
  Club of the Year £300. Community-run (not the GAA); prize amounts vary by season — flagged.

### 8. Louisiana Film Prize (Prize Fest) — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `prizefest.com/film/rules/` (official).
- **Verified:** "the largest cash prize for a short film in the world — $50,000"; entry fee $0.
  Principal photography must be in Louisiana (Caddo/Bossier for $50,000, elsewhere for $25,000);
  each of 20 finalists gets $250.

### 9. Pensacola Beach Songwriters Festival Competition — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `pensacolabeachsongwritersfestival.com/2026-songwriting-competition/` (official).
- **Verified:** "FREE to enter… NO FEE." Grand prize $1,000 + festival performance; category winners
  $150. US residents only.

### 10. Young Lions Fiction Award (NYPL) — `live`, `full` ✅ (official)
- **Source:** `nypl.org/about/awards/young-lions-fiction-award/submission-form` (official).
- **Verified:** $10,000 for the best fiction book by a US author 35-or-under; no entry fee, but
  publisher-submitted and book must be published in the calendar year.
