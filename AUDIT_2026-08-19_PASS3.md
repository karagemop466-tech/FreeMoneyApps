## 1. ❌❌ Critical: `lucktastic` is a dead app, listed as live and **featured**

`lucktastic.com` now **301s to `mobilityware.com`**, a Solitaire publisher.
Chasing it down:

- MobilityWare acquired Jump Ramp Games (Lucktastic's owner) in **Sept 2019**.
- **Lucktastic shut down permanently on 16 March 2022.** Play Store listing:
  *"This app is currently not available on Google Play."* User reviews from 2022 discuss
  the retirement and forfeited token balances.
- Crunchbase lists Jump Ramp Games Inc — *"Also Known As: Jump Ramp Games, Lucktastic"* —
  with **Operating Status: Closed**.
- The entry's `tosUrl`/`rulesUrl` (`m.lucktastic.com/terms-conditions`) **fails to load**.

The ledger nevertheless has it as:

```
status: "live"        freePath: "full"        lastChecked: "2026-08-12"
featured: [... "lucktastic" ...]
minCashout: "$5 cash wallet (ToS). Gift cards require a large token balance."
signupSteps: ["Install Lucktastic from the App Store or open lucktastic.com", ...]
verification.steps: ["Large wins ($100+) have a short claim window (ToS: 7 days)..."]
```

This is the one place I'd use the word the user asked about. The entry doesn't just have a
stale status — it carries **specific operational detail cited to a ToS that cannot be
retrieved, for an app that has not existed since 2022**, and the repo puts it on the
front page as a featured pick. Whatever the provenance, it should be treated as
unsourced and pulled immediately.

## 2. ❌ `aarp` — right claim, wrong URL (also featured)

`aarp.org/benefits-discounts/sweepstakes/` **redirects to a generic member-benefits
directory** with no sweepstakes on it. So the cited source supports nothing.

The *claim*, though, checks out against AARP's own eProize-hosted official rules:
$1,500 weekly checks + a $5,000 week-eight prize (Spring Sweeps); $1,000 weekly wire
transfers (Games Throwback); a $26,000 check ("$26,000 Your Way", ended 15 May 2026).
Ledger's "commonly $1,500–$10,000" is **directionally accurate ✅**.

Fix: repoint to `aarp.org/getrewards/campaigns/...`. Claim stands.

## 6. What holds up

Worth stating plainly, because it's most of the repo: **no fabricated operators, anywhere.**
Every company, app, league and contest named here is or was real. Where the ledger cites a
primary source, accuracy is high and often exact — PCH's official rules, CBS's
$1K/$5K/$100K structure, ESPN's $102K/58 and $86K/46, HGTV's $750,000 cash option, UEFA's
prize ladder, Fantasy EFL's 2026/27 table, FFScout's four leagues, and every sweeps-casino
redemption minimum. The schema is clean: no duplicate IDs, no dangling references, no
future dates, all enum values valid, 119 listings across 9 categories as README claims.
