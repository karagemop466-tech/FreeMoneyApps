## Entries added — source, tier, and what was verified

### 1. NASCAR Fantasy Live — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `fantasygames.nascar.com/live/page/rules` (official; fetched in full this pass).
- **Verified:** Grand Prize $25,000 check, Second $10,000, Third $5,000, Daytona 500 Champion
  $10,000. IRS Form 1099 issued. Eligibility 50 US + DC, 18+. No purchase necessary, 5 entries max.

### 2. PGA TOUR Fantasy Golf — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `fantasygolf.pgatour.com/prizes` + `pgatour.com/pages/whats-new` (official).
- **Verified:** total ARV of all prizes $5,000 (overall winner $1,000 gift card, runner-up
  $500, second runner-up $300, four segment winners $500 / runners-up $300). 2026 adds weekly
  single-tournament trip prizes. Non-cash. Note: the /prizes URL currently returns a client-side
  "Page Not Found" (seasonal), so the figure is carried from the official page as indexed.

### 3. NHL Stanley Cup Playoffs Bracket Challenge — `ended` (returns 2027) ✅
- **Source:** `bracketchallenge.nhl.com/en/how-to-play` + `nhl.com/news/stanley-cup-playoffs-second-chance-bracket` (official).
- **Verified:** Grand Prize = 2026/27 NHL event package + Norqain watch, ARV up to $10,490 USD;
  Second Chance bracket $1,000 NHL Shop gift code. ~1.33M brackets in 2026. CA winners face a skill test.

### 4. ESPN NBA Playoff Challenge — `ended` (returns 2027) ✅
- **Source:** `fantasy.espn.com/games/nba-playoff-challenge-2026/howtoplay` (official).
- **Verified:** Total Prizes $50,000, max 2 entries, event dates 18 Apr – 19 Jun 2026.

### 5. NBA Pick'Em — `ended` (returns Oct) ✅
- **Source:** two `nba.com/news` pages (official).
- **Verified:** perfect bracket $1,000,000; first prize courtside tickets; other prizes signed
  memorabilia + $100–$500 NBAStore.com credits. "Streak to the Finals" grand prize = trip to
  2026 Finals Game 3. Only the perfect-bracket $1M is cash. US & Canada only.

### 6. Bundesliga Fantasy Manager — `live` ✅
- **Source:** two `bundesliga.com` news pages + App Store (developer DFL) (official).
- **Verified:** prizes = Bundesliga match trip, VIP Supercup access, DERBYSTAR balls, signed
  jerseys; FC 27 League auto-enters all players for 50 EA SPORTS FC 27 game codes after MD4.
  Non-cash.

### 7. La Liga Fantasy — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `laliga.com/en-GB/legal/condiciones-de-uso-fantasy` (official terms).
- **Verified:** free to access; "There will be no cash prizes or alternative prizes."
  Sponsored League prizes restricted to Spain-region users; identity checks possible.

### 8. Swagbucks Daily Trivia — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `help.swagbucks.com` article + App Store listing (Prodege LLC) (official).
- **Verified:** Daily Trivia Live (Mon–Thu) + Daily Trivia Challenge (daily); winnings paid in
  SB, redeemed for PayPal cash or gift cards. Red flag recorded: per-game wins are cents, not
  the "$1000s every week" marketing headline (a pot shared across all players).

### 9. 99designs Design Contests — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `support.99designs.com` "What is a design contest?" (official).
- **Verified:** free to create an account and enter contests; client funds the prize; winning
  designer receives prize money and transfers copyright; platform fee (~5–15%) deducted.

### 10. Freelancer.com Contests — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `freelancer.com/faq/topic.php?id=33` (official FAQ).
- **Verified:** "entering a contest is free for all contest participants"; winner charged 10%
  of prize or $5 (whichever greater) on handover; prize auto-released on entry acceptance.

### 11. Battlefy Esports Tournaments — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `help.battlefy.com` "Where is my prize?" (official).
- **Verified:** free-to-enter and paid tournaments; "Battlefy does not provide or guarantee
  prizing as we do not operate events" — prizes come from organizers. Listed as `partial` free path.

### 12. Challengermode — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `support.challengermode.com` "How to get started" + organizers page (official).
- **Verified:** "Most tournaments are free to enter"; win money and other prizes; winnings
  credited instantly and withdrawable; paid competitions optional. 200+ game titles. `partial` free path.
