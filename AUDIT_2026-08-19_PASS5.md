## Entries added — source, tier, and what was verified

### 1. ATP Fantasy (Official ATP Tour) — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `atptour.com/en/news/atp-launches-official-fantasy-game-2026` (official).
- **Verified:** free; 23 tournament weeks Apr–Nov 2026; prizes = ATP partner/Store merchandise and
  2027 tickets; overall winner gets two tickets to two 2027 Nitto ATP Finals sessions (flights +
  accommodation). Non-cash.

### 2. F1 Fantasy (Official Formula 1) — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `formula1.com/.../boost-the-fun...f1-fantasy-is-back` (official).
- **Verified:** free, $100m cost cap, five drivers + two constructors, up to three teams. Global
  leaderboard prizes: 1st = 2027 F1 tickets (GP of choice + VIP incl. grid walk), 2nd = two
  grandstand tickets, 3rd = £500 F1 Store voucher. Non-cash.

### 3. Esports World Cup (EWC) Play — Fantasy & Pick'em — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `esportsworldcup.com/en/play/fantasy` + `/en/news/play-fantasy-at-EWC` (official).
- **Verified (fetched):** "It's free. There's no entry fee and no wager or stake of any kind."
  Fantasy leaderboard pays 1st $10,000 / 2nd $7,000 / 3rd $6,000 / 4th $5,000, "Top 130 paid";
  2026 announced a $200,000 pool ($100k Pick'em / $100k Fantasy). Cash prizes 18+ with eligibility
  checks. EWC 2026 = Paris, 6 Jul – 23 Aug; returns summer 2027.

### 4. ESPN Playoff Hockey Challenge — `ended` (returns 2027) ✅
- **Source:** `fantasy.espn.com/games/nhl-playoff-hockey-challenge-2026/group` (official).
- **Verified:** Total Prizes $10,000, max 2 entries, 18 Apr – 21 Jun 2026. Distinct from the NHL's
  own Bracket Challenge (already listed).

### 5. ESPN College Football Playoff Bracket Challenge — `ended` (returns Dec 2026) ✅
- **Source:** `fantasy.espn.com/games/college-football-playoff-challenge-2025/mostpickedchampions` (official).
- **Verified:** $50,000 in prizes, max 2 entries, 19 Dec 2025 – 19 Jan 2026.

### 6. ESPN Knockout Bracket Challenge (World Cup) — `ended` (returns 2030) ✅
- **Source:** `fantasy.espn.com/games/mens-knockout-bracket-challenge-2026/howtoplay` (official).
- **Verified:** $10,000, up to 25 brackets, 28 Jun – 19 Jul 2026 (World Cup 2026 knockout stage).

### 7. MLB Home Run Derby Pick'Em (T-Mobile) — `ended` (returns Jul 2027) ✅
- **Source:** `mlb.com/sponsorship/home-run-derby-pick-em/official-rules` (official).
- **Verified (fetched):** grand prize $100,000 (perfect 100-point bracket); first prize a World
  Series trip (ARV $6,500) if nobody hits 100 but someone scores 55+. **Honesty note recorded:**
  the rules page currently reflects the 2024 edition (entry period 10–15 Jul 2024); the figure is
  real but should be re-confirmed when the 2027 edition opens.

### 8. TradingView — The Leap — `live` (periodic) ✅
- **Source:** `tradingview.com/the-leap/tradestation-july-2026/rules/` (official).
- **Verified (fetched):** total cash ARV $50,000 — 1st $10,000 / 2nd $7,000 / 3rd $6,000 / 4th
  $3,500 / 5th $2,500 / 6–25 $550 / 26–50 $400 / 51–250 3-month subscription. Paid via wire/PayPal
  (≥$1,000) or PayPal (<$1,000). Free with $100,000 virtual money. Periodic editions.

### 9. Wall Street Survivor Trading Contests — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `wallstreetsurvivor.com/contest-valentines-2026/` (official).
- **Verified:** free to join, no credit card, $100,000 virtual cash, monthly themed contests; top
  three win Amazon gift cards (typically $300 / $150 / $50 = $500 total). Gift cards, not cash.

### 10. Start.gg (formerly smash.gg) — `live` ✅
- **Source:** `start.gg/` (official).
- **Verified:** self-service esports bracket platform (Microsoft-acquired 2020, independent Jan
  2025). Community brackets are often free to enter with organizer-funded prize pools; the platform
  does not itself guarantee prizes. `partial` free path.
