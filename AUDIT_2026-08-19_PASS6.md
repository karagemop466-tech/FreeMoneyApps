## Entries added — source, tier, and what was verified

### 1. Chess.com Prize Tournaments — `live`, `partial` ✅
- **Source:** `chess.com/announcements` + `support.chess.com` (official).
- **Verified:** official prize-tournament list = Titled Tuesday, Arena Kings, Variants Community
  Series, Bullet Brawls. Arena Kings up to $2,500/month; variant championships $2,500–$5,000.
  **Honesty note:** cash tiers gated (Titled Tuesday = titled-only; Arena Kings = streamers);
  open community events pay credits/Diamond memberships, not cash.

### 2. Play! Pokémon Championship Series — `live`, `partial` ✅
- **Source:** `pokemon.com/us/play-pokemon` + `support.pokemon.com` (official).
- **Verified:** official support page states "Organizers may charge a fee to participate" and
  Regional/International Championships award USD prize money (cash / scholarship / Visa prepaid).
  **Honesty note:** upper tiers charge entry fees; free local League Challenges pay Championship
  Points, not money. Specific cash figures (Liquipedia) marked indicative/community, not
  operator-published.

### 3. DesignCrowd Design Contests — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `designcrowd.com/help` (official).
- **Verified:** Community Contests are "completely free to participate," including monetary-prize
  ones; marketplace contests are free to enter with a success fee only on winning.

### 4. CrowdSpring Design Contests — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `crowdspring.com` (official).
- **Verified:** same contest model (client funds prize, designers enter free, winner paid minus
  platform fee). No specific dollar figure asserted — prize varies per brief.

### 5. ESPN Women's Tournament Challenge — `ended` (returns Mar 2027), `full` ✅
- **Source:** `fantasy.espn.com/games/tournament-challenge-bracket-women-2026` (official).
- **Verified:** Total Prizes $125,000, 25 brackets, 20 Mar – 5 Apr 2026.

### 6. Yahoo Fantasy Bracket Mayhem — `ended` (returns Mar 2027), `full` ✅
- **Source:** `sports.yahoo.com` (official).
- **Verified:** best men's bracket $25,000 + best women's bracket $25,000 = $50,000 total, free.

### 7. BullRush Trading Competitions — `live`, `partial` ✅
- **Source:** `bullrush.com/competition-types` + `bullrush.com/terms-and-conditions` (official).
- **Verified:** hourly competitions 100% free to enter; monthly $100K+ tournament. **Honesty note:**
  top prizes are funded prop accounts + a share of the prize pool, not direct cash; KYC to withdraw;
  some tiers are paid-entry.

### 8. Signate (Japan) — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `signate.jp` + `x.com/signatelab` (official).
- **Verified:** Japan's data-science competition platform; official account advertises a NEDO
  contest with ¥4,000,000 total / ¥1,200,000 top. ML Contests logged ~$25,000 in 2025. Yen-denominated.

### 9. Tianchi (Alibaba Cloud) — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `tianchi.aliyun.com` (official).
- **Verified:** example competition pays Gold $3,000 / Silver $2,000 / Bronze $1,000 + Alibaba
  internships; larger contests reach ¥1,000,000 (~$157,000). ML Contests: $1,028,000 across 51
  competitions in 2025 (second-largest platform after Kaggle).

### 10. Hypermind Forecasting Contests — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `dash.hypermind.com` (official).
- **Verified:** "free to play, with cash rewards"; 200,000 play-money starting capital; rewards
  split pro-rata of forecasting performance; withdrawable as **Amazon gift certificates**
  (min $20/€15). 24,573€ at stake at review. Run by Lumenogic (Good Judgment Project lineage).
