## Entries added — source, tier, and what was verified

- **Source:** `help.fundednext.com` (official help centre).
- **Verified:** "completely free," no entry fee; $100,000 simulated account; top three win cash
  ($5,000 / $2,000 / $1,000) + a Stellar account; ranks 4–600 get funded accounts. Honesty note:
  demo profits can't be withdrawn — only the prize (cash or funded account) is real.

### 2. For Traders Tournaments — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `fortraders.com/tournaments` + official X account (official).
- **Verified:** free Wednesday Clash (24h forex), Weekend Crypto Clash (72h crypto), monthly comps;
  "completely free with no deposit." Official account advertised a $75,000 Wednesday Clash prize
  pool paid as funded-account challenges. Prizes are funded accounts, not cash.

### 3. NinjaTrader Arena — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `ninjatrader.com/ninjatrader-arena/` (official).
- **Verified:** free simulated futures competitions on live CME data; winners get "real cash prizes
  or account credits, depending on the competition format."

### 4. Yahoo Daily Draw — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `help.yahoo.com` KB SLN36907 / SLN36915 (official).
- **Verified:** free-to-play prediction game (pick 4 of 6 cards); $500 daily / $1,000 weekly prize
  pools; prizes issued as bank eGift cards; administered by GMR Marketing. Non-cash (gift cards).

### 5. Fantasy DP World Tour — `live`, `full` ✅
- **Source:** `europeantour.com` (official).
- **Verified:** free; overall 1st = Callaway clubs set + custom fitting; Rolex Series hospitality;

### 6. The Open Fantasy (Match Ups) — `ended` (returns Jul 2027), `full` ✅

### 7. The Sun Dream Team — `live`, `full` ✅

### 8. Sky Sports Fantasy Football — `live`, `full` ✅
