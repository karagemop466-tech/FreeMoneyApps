# Free Money & Contest Ledger

A curated, audited list of **free-to-enter competitions that can pay real money** — sweepstakes,
social casinos, trivia, fantasy and pick'em games, sportsbook free-to-play, prediction contests,
judged prizes, and pool-hosting tools.

**[View the live list →](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/FreeMoneyApps/)**

## What's here

- **119 listings** across 9 categories, rendered as one easy-to-read, grouped list.
- Every entry has an **official link** (signup or official rules) plus supporting sources.
- Honest stamps: **free USD path**, **free + AMOE**, **some free events**, or **cash needs entry fee** — so gems, tickets, and bonus cash are never dressed up as dollars.
- Verification stack shown per entry (account only → eligibility → full KYC → tax forms).
- No search box, no filter stack — just scroll or tap a section to jump.

## Where the data lives

- `index.html` — the page.
- `assets/data.js` — the single source of truth (`window.LEDGER.listings`).
- `assets/js/app.js` — renders the grouped list.
- `assets/css/style.css` — styling.

Supporting research docs: `index.md` (earlier NFL/EPL list) and `100_VERIFIED_CONTESTS.md` (100 skill-competition sweep).

## Re-review checklist

Before adding or re-opening an entry in `assets/data.js`:

1. Still on the App Store / resolving at the official domain?
2. Official Rules still say no purchase necessary?
3. Today's cash minimum, playthrough, and payout method?
4. Newly blocked states or countries?
5. Did KYC or tax forms change?
6. Fresh, specific cashout complaints (not generic "rigged" posts)?
7. Update `lastChecked` and the source list.
