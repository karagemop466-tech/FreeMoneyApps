Desk rules

How every listing is reviewed.

Compiled 12 August 2026 from official terms, App Store listings, and public threads on r/beermoney, r/sweepstakes, r/Sweepstake, and r/EarnExtraIncome. The goal is a ledger you can audit — not a ranking bought by affiliate copy.

What gets in

  1. There is a competition: drawing, tournament, judged contest, trivia pot, or redeemable sweeps play. Pure survey / offer walls (Swagbucks, Freecash, JustPlay) are out of scope unless they run a separate contest.
  2. There is a free entry path, or the listing is stamped “cash needs entry fee” so nobody confuses Gem rooms with dollars.
  3. A winner can receive real USD (PayPal, bank, check, Stripe, or 1:1 redeemable sweeps currency). Gift-card-only token sinks are noted, not celebrated.
  4. Signup exists on iOS and/or a browser. Android-only products are omitted unless they used to be on iOS and need a closed file (Lucky Day).

What we open before a stamp goes on

  • Official rules / Sweeps Rules / Terms of Use (eligibility, AMOE, prize forfeiture).
  • Cashier or FAQ for minimum redemption, playthrough, and payout rails.
  • App Store listing for iOS presence and what the developer actually claims.
  • Recent forum or news mentions for shutdowns, geo blocks, and cashout walls.

Status stamps

  • Active — still running a free or AMOE path to USD as of the review date.
  • Mixed — real USD exists, but most inventory is paid or membership-gated.
  • Caution — still live, but economics or regulators have raised serious flags (Givling).
  • Inactive / closed — gone from iOS, off-air, or not a responsible recommendation (Lucky Day, HQ Trivia).

Forum notes that shaped the desk

  • r/beermoney has asked for a single free-contest collector for years. Regular answers: Online-Sweepstakes.com, SweepstakesToday, Sweeties Sweeps.
  • r/sweepstakes and r/giveaways are the live firehose for brand cash (ViralSweep / Gleam). They also attract casino-affiliate spam — hence the red-flag line on those cards.
  • r/EarnExtraIncome 2026 threads push Blitz / Bingo Cash / Solitaire Cash. Those are skill-cash products. We list them as paid-cash so the catalog does not launder ads.
  • Papaya ToS (Feb 2026) and FAQ are unusually clear: Gems competitions are free; cash competitions take a platform fee from entry pools; Bonus Cash dies on withdrawal.

Re-review checklist

Use this when you reopen a card, or when you add a new one to assets/data.js:

  1. Is the product still on the App Store or resolving at the official domain?
  2. Does the current Official Rules page still say no purchase necessary?
  3. What is today’s cash minimum, playthrough, and payout method?
  4. Which states or countries are newly blocked?
  5. Did KYC docs or tax forms change?
  6. Are there fresh, specific cashout complaints (not generic “rigged” posts)?
  7. Update lastChecked and the source list.

What this ledger is not

  • Not legal advice, tax advice, or a guarantee that a listed app will pay you.
  • Not a complete universe — brand sweeps turn over weekly; use the directory cards.
  • Not an endorsement of gambling. Sweeps casinos are chance products with a house edge.
